My name is Dingdong LIU. I'm currently a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) at the [HCI Initiative](https://hci.cse.ust.hk/) of [HKUST](https://hkust.edu.hk/), supervised by Prof. [Xiaojuan MA](https://seng.hkust.edu.hk/about/people/faculty/xiaojuan-ma) and Prof. [Fugee TSUNG](https://seng.hkust.edu.hk/about/people/faculty/fugee-tsung) since 2022. I also do research on humanoid robots with Prof. [Bertram Emil SHI](https://seng.hkust.edu.hk/about/people/faculty/bertram-emil-shi) at the [Center for Aging Science](https://aging.hkust.edu.hk/). Prior to that, I earned my Bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Data Science at HKUST.

My work asks how conversational agents can acquire **communicative competence** — the ability to hold a conversation the way a skilled human interlocutor would. My thesis approaches this at two levels:

- **Coordination** — reading conversational dynamics: when to speak, how to signal and yield the floor, and how to handle interruptions. This is where my work on [embodied spoken dialogue systems and humanoid robots](/publications/) sits.
- **Content** — managing what the conversation is *about*: scaffolding questions for users who need support, switching topics, and steering medical interviews toward the information that matters.

Together these ask what an agent must be able to do to genuinely support people — in clinical interviews, cognitive screening, and everyday decision making.
